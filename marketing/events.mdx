## Overview

This page walks through how we plan, promote, and run events — customer/prospect events, recruiting events, and general brand-awareness events.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the goal">
    Start by identifying why we're hosting this event. Common goals:

    - Engaging customers
    - Engaging prospects
    - Recruiting
    - General brand awareness

    The goal shapes the budget owner, the guest list, and the promotion strategy below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the budget">
    - **Marketing budget** — confirm with Lauren Volpi
    - **Recruiting budget** — confirm with Lauren Volpi and/or Shawn

    <Note>
      Any major contract needs Shawn's signature. Lauren Volpi can sign off on smaller contracts — use judgment based on the size of the commitment.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book the venue and food">
    Once the budget is confirmed:

    - Book a venue that fits the budget
    - Decide on food — either provided by the venue or catered separately
    - Keep food, drinks, venue, and decorations all within budget
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort out decorations and merch">
    We have reusable decorations in the office (tablecloths, plates, merch, etc.).

    - Check with Andressa to confirm we have the right inventory for what you want
    - If we don't have what you need, work with Andressa to order it
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the event in Luma">
    - Create the event in Luma (request access from Han if you don't already have it)
    - Copy the event details over to the company events calendar
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm team support">
    - Line up team members to support the event based on the nature of the event
    - Han and Hahnbee typically tag-team event support, but generally don't both attend unless the event specifically calls for it
    - Make sure you have enough coverage overall, picking the right people for the event
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote the event">
    - Share that we're hosting in the events Slack channel
    - Work with marketing (HK and Cole) on email and/or ads, depending on the event:
      - Email prospects or people who've signed up
      - Advertise locally via LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.
    - If it's an open event, work with HK to get it posted as a LinkedIn event
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coordinate invites">
    - **Customer/prospect events** — work with the sales team to invite people
    - **Recruiting events** — align with Lauren on an invite strategy
    - Send a reminder email to all attendees through Luma before the event

    <Tip>
      Invite more people than you have room for — expect a 40–60% drop-off rate.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final coordination">
    - Confirm everything you need for the day of the event
    - Show up early
    - Make sure all payments (cards, etc.) are set up ahead of time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day of the event">
    - Check people in as they arrive so we know who actually showed up
    - Focus on hosting a great event
    - Clean up and bring any extra supplies back to the office
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    - Put all receipts into Ramp
    - Share the list of attendees:
      - With sales, for more intimate customer/prospect events (e.g. dinners)
      - With Lauren, for recruiting events — flag anyone you had a good conversation with
  </Step>
</Steps>
